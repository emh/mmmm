Animation Bench

Every sprite is normalised onto one canvas at one body scale (tools/normalize.py). The grid lines are shared, so any drift in size or footing shows up as frames that disagree. The dashed red line is each frame's measured ground.

Standing, and the transitions out of it

Standing is the anchor pose every transition begins from. Each clip plays once and holds on its final frame; replay runs it again, reverse plays it backwards — which is how the return trip (sitting → standing) comes free. All frames are registered on the ground line, not the spine: her feet stay planted while the body drops.

Gaits — walk, trot, gallop

All three generated from one three-row sheet (art/prompts/gaits-away.txt), cut with tools/gridcut.py. Each plays at a speed appropriate to its gait. The faint version behind each is every frame of that cycle overlaid — a sharp body with only the legs blurred means a usable cycle.
