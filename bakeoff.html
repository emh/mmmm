Mighty Miss Molly Mae

Style bake-off · PRD §16.3
Decided 26 Aug 2026 — Candidate A (naturalist field guide). Kept as the record of that decision; see PRD §16.

The two candidates converged at high quality. At low quality B was visibly softer and flatter; more compute made the model render harder, and rendering pulls toward naturalism. B's central argument — cheaper and simpler to animate — turned out to be unavailable through this pipeline, so A is partly a default rather than purely a merit win. Worth reading §16 before reopening this.

Six assets per candidate. Molly Mae is a straight-coat labradoodle in both — a curly coat is off-model — and wears no collar.

Backdrops are crops of design/art-environment.png, standing in until real environment art exists. The question is whether Molly reads without any outline or marker.

Judging criteria used

  1. At phone size, can you tell the four states apart without labels?
  2. Does she read as the same dog across all six assets?
  3. Is she findable against the forest without any UI marker?
  4. Does fear read as alert plus posture, given there is no frightened face?
  5. Which style could plausibly be extended to a full set at this budget?
